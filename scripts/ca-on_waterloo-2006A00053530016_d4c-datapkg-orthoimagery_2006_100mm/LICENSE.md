## Waterloo Open Data User Licence

### Background on the licence

Open data licences are still evolving through local to international discussions on best practices. The licence adapted, with permission, by the City originates from the U.K. Government's licence. This licence was adapted by the British Columbia government as well as several municipalities including the City of Toronto and the Region of Waterloo. For sake of consistency and benefit to the user community, the City of Waterloo has used the same licence.

There are ongoing discussions on an enhanced licence for all levels of government within Canada and the City will participate in these discussions and will provide updates on any new versions that are proposed for community feedback.

If you have any questions or concerns related to the current licence, please contact us.
